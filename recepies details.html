Chicken Biryani

A delicious and aromatic Indian rice dish prepared with flavorful spices and tender chicken.

⏱ 60 minutes ⭐ 4.8

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Ginger Garlic Paste
  • Biryani Masala
  • Fresh Coriander

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with spices.
  3. Cook onions and spices in a pan.
  4. Add chicken and cook properly.
  5. Add rice and cook until soft.
  6. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.
